# Privacy Policy for Thirst of the Immortal

**Effective Date:** November 11, 2026

**DT Squared Media** ("we," "our," or "us") built the **Thirst of the Immortal** mobile application as a commercial/free game. This page is used to inform visitors and users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use our Service.

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**Thirst of the Immortal** does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or user data.

* We do not require you to create an account or log in.
* We do not track your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
* All game progress and save data are stored locally on your device.

### 2. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We do not share any user data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics companies. The application is built using the Unity game engine, operating offline/locally without external data-harvesting SDKs enabled.

### 3. Children’s Privacy

Our Service does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected or transmitted by the application, we are compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements.

### 4. Security

Because we do not collect or transmit your personal data to external servers, your gameplay information remains exclusively on your local device.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time (for example, if we add online leaderboards or cloud saves in future updates). Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
